SENTENCE 1 — Paraphrase
The table compares / illustrates / presents data on + [TOPIC] + across [N] categories / for [N] countries
SENTENCE 2 — Overview (NO numbers!)
Overall, / In general, + [X] ranks highest / leads in all categories + while [Y] lags far behind
Top 10 Must-Use Structures
1
[X] ranked highest in [category], at [figure] Norway ranked highest in GDP, at $82,500.
2
[X] substantially outpaced [Y] in [category] Norway substantially outpaced Ethiopia in internet access.
3
… ranged from [low] to [high] Life expectancy ranged from 66.2 to 82.5 years.
4
[X] was approximately [N] times greater than [Y] Norway's GDP was ~90 times greater than Ethiopia's.
5
With the exception of [X], [trend] With the exception of Ethiopia, literacy was high.
6
While [X] and [Y] [shared feature], [contrast] While Brazil and India shared similar life expectancy, their GDPs differed greatly.
7
In descending order, figures stood at [A, B, C, D] In descending order, internet access stood at 98%, 70%, 43%, 15%.
8
Shifting focus to [category], [observation] Shifting focus to social indicators, the picture becomes more nuanced.
9
[X] proved a notable outlier, recording [figure] Ethiopia proved a notable outlier, recording just 38% literacy.
10
… reflecting each [subject]'s level of [development] …reflecting each country's level of economic development.
Tense Rule
PAST Table has a specific year → simple past: "Norway had..."
PRESENT No year given → simple present: "Norway ranks..."

5 Deadly Mistakes — Avoid These
✕ Numbers in your overview → Overview = trends only, no figures
✕ Row-by-row description → Group by tier or category
✕ Describing every data point → Select the 5–6 most significant
✕ Mixing past and present tense → One tense throughout (see rule ←)
✕ also / also / also (×5) → Vary: similarly · moreover · in contrast · notably
3-Min Pre-Writing Checklist
Circled the highest & lowest value in each column
Found the single biggest gap (your multiplier target)
Grouped data into 2 tiers or 2 categories
Spotted any outlier for exception clause
Decided overview pattern (winner · gap · divide)
Checked tense (year present? → past)
