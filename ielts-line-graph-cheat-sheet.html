📈 Line Graph Cheat Sheet

Your Exam Day Toolkit - Review in 10 Minutes!

THE WINNING FORMULA

Intro: Paraphrase + Overview (NO DATA!) → Para 2: First Trend (starting point → movement → endpoint) → Para 3: Second Trend (contrast + compare)

Top 10 Must-Use Structures

INTRO The [graph/chart] illustrates [changes/trends in...] over [time period]
OVERVIEW Overall, it is clear that [main trend], while [contrasting trend]
START At the outset, [subject] stood at [figure]
TREND Over the following [period], [subject] experienced a [adjective] surge/increase/decline
DATA [Subject] climbed from [X] in [year] to [Y] in [year]
COMPARE In contrast to [X], where [trend], [Y] showed [opposite trend]
COMPARE While [X] rose to [%], [Y] recorded a more modest increase to [%]
PEAK [Subject] reached a peak of [figure] in [year], before declining to [figure]
END By the end of the period, [subject] had reached/stood at [figure]
GROUP Both [X] and [Y] followed similar trends, rising by approximately [%] and [%] respectively

20 Power Collocations

experience a dramatic surge
witness a sharp increase

📉 FALLING TRENDS

🔍 COMPARISON

⚡ LAST-MINUTE REMINDERS - READ BEFORE ENTERING!

  • Overview = NO DATA! Only trends/patterns
  • Use "from X to Y" for changes
  • Vary verbs: rose/climbed/surged/increased
  • Time markers: "By 2020, sales HAD reached..."
  • Group similar trends in one sentence
  • Use "approximately/just over" for precision
  • Never say "we/you/I" in Task 1
  • Include starting + ending figures
  • Compare lines - don't just describe each
  • 150+ words = aim for 170-180 to be safe
