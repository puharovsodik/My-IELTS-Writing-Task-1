⚡ IELTS PROCESS DIAGRAM: EXAM DAY CHEAT SHEET

10-Minute Review Toolkit | Band 7-9 Essentials

🎯 TOP 10 MUST-USE STRUCTURES

1. INTRODUCTION: The [diagram] illustrates [how X is produced]. Overall, the process comprises [#] stages, commencing with [first step] and culminating in [final step].
2. BASIC PASSIVE: [Material] is/are [past participle] → The beans are roasted at 180°C.
3. TRANSFORMATION: [X] is converted/transformed into [Y] → Cocoa beans are converted into chocolate.
4. SEQUENCE WITH DETAIL: [Transition], [material] is [verb] [specific detail] → Subsequently, the mixture is heated to 1400°C in a rotating kiln.
5. PASSIVE CHAIN: [X] is [verb1], [verb2], and then [verb3] → The liquid is filtered, cooled, and then stored.
6. TIME CLAUSE: Once/After [process] is complete, [next step] → Once cooling is complete, packaging begins.
7. PURPOSE: [Material] is [verb] to [purpose] → Materials are filtered to remove impurities.
8. RESULT (THEREBY): [Action], thereby [verb-ing] [result] → Materials are heated rapidly, thereby reducing production time.
9. PARALLEL PROCESS: While [X] is being [verb], [Y] simultaneously undergoes [process] → While one batch is heated, another undergoes chemical treatment.
10. ADVANCED (BAND 8+): [Material] is subjected to [process], during which [detail] → The mixture is subjected to intense pressure, during which air bubbles are removed.

✅ 60-SECOND EXAM CHECKLIST

Before Writing

  • Count exact # of stages
  • Note specific details (temps, times)
  • Identify parallel processes

While Writing

  • Use PASSIVE voice
  • Include ALL details from diagram
  • Vary your connectors
  • Write 150-190 words

Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Active voice ("workers do")
  • ❌ Repeating "then/next"
  • ❌ Missing specific numbers
  • ❌ Wrong tense (use present)
