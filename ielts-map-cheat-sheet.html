⚡ IELTS MAP TASK 1: EXAM DAY TOOLKIT ⚡

Top 10 Structures + 20 Power Collocations | Review in 10 Minutes Before Your Test

🎯 TOP 10 MUST-USE STRUCTURES
1
The [maps/diagrams] illustrate how [area] has transformed over [period].
2
Overall, the area underwent substantial development, with [change 1] and [change 2].
3
[Feature] was replaced by [new feature].
4
[Building] was constructed to the north/south/east/west of [location].
5
Several [facilities] were built, including [X], [Y], and [Z].
6
While [area 1] underwent [change], [area 2] remained unchanged.
7
[Old building] was converted into [new purpose].
8
By [year], [feature] had been constructed.
9
A [feature] was added, connecting [location A] to [location B].
10
Conversely, the [area/feature] remained largely unchanged.

💡 QUICK EXAM TIPS
✍️
PASSIVE VOICE
Use "was built" NOT "they built"
📊
OVERVIEW FIRST
Start with overall change in para 1
🎯
BE SPECIFIC
Add locations: "to the north of"
🔄
SHOW CONTRAST
Use "while" / "conversely"
⏱️
PAST TENSE
Use past simple for all changes
📝
GROUP CHANGES
"Several facilities including..."
