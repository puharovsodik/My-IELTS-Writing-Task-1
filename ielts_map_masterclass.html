Welcome, future Band 7+ achiever! Maps are unique in IELTS Task 1 because they test your ability to describe spatial changes, locations, and transformations. Unlike graphs with numbers, maps require specific vocabulary for directions, positions, and developments. This guide will give you the exact sentence structures, vocabulary, and techniques that consistently score high marks.

1 PART 1: INTRODUCTION TEMPLATES

Your introduction has TWO jobs: (1) paraphrase the question, and (2) write an overview statement. Let's master both!

1.1 Paraphrasing the Question

The Secret:
Change keywords WITHOUT changing the meaning. Maps typically show changes over time OR compare two different periods/versions.

TEMPLATE 1: For time-based changes

The [maps/diagrams] illustrate how [area name] has transformed/developed/changed over [time period].
EXAMPLE 1:
Original: The maps show how Westley village changed between 1990 and 2020.
Your paraphrase: The diagrams illustrate how Westley village has transformed over a 30-year period from 1990 to 2020.
GRAMMAR BREAKDOWN
The diagrams
Subject → What you're describing
illustrate
Verb → show/depict/present (use present simple)
how Westley village
What changed
has transformed
Present perfect → showing change from past to now
over a 30-year period
Time reference → be specific!
COMMON MISTAKE
❌ Don't say: "The maps show the changes of Westley village."
Why wrong? "Changes of" is unnatural. Use "how X has changed" or "changes in X."

TEMPLATE 2: For comparing two locations/plans

The [maps/plans] compare [two versions/layouts] of [location name], showing [the main difference].
EXAMPLE 2:
The plans compare two proposed designs for a city center redevelopment, showing how the layout would differ between the residential-focused and commercial-focused options.

1.2 Overview Statements (The Game-Changer!)

This is your secret weapon!
The overview gives the BIG PICTURE in 1-2 sentences. It's worth serious marks, so NEVER skip it.

For maps, your overview should mention:

  • The OVERALL change (became more developed/urbanized/industrialized)
  • What was ADDED or REMOVED
  • What STAYED THE SAME (if relevant)

OVERVIEW TEMPLATE 1: Describing development

Overall, the area underwent substantial development/transformation, with [main change 1] and [main change 2], while [something that stayed the same] remained unchanged.
EXAMPLE 3:
Overall, the area underwent substantial urbanization, with numerous residential buildings constructed and farmland replaced by infrastructure, while the river running through the center remained unchanged.

OVERVIEW TEMPLATE 2: For replacement/demolition

The most notable changes include the demolition of [old feature] and the construction of [new feature], transforming the area from [previous character] to [new character].
EXAMPLE 4:
The most notable changes include the demolition of agricultural buildings and the construction of a shopping complex and parking facilities, transforming the area from a rural village to a suburban commercial hub.

2 PART 2: KEY DESCRIPTION VOCABULARY PATTERNS

Maps need LOCATION vocabulary (where things are) and TRANSFORMATION vocabulary (what changed). Master these 30 examiner-approved phrases!

2.1 Location & Position Vocabulary

THE POWER PHRASES (Use these to score 7+):

Phrase Meaning Example Sentence
to the north/south/east/west of Compass direction A school was built to the north of the park.
in the northern/southern corner Specific corner location Houses appeared in the northwestern corner.
adjacent to / next to Immediately beside A library was constructed adjacent to the town hall.
opposite / facing Across from The cinema was located opposite the shopping mall.
situated/located in General position The factory was situated in the industrial zone.
on both sides of Flanking a feature Shops lined on both sides of the main road.
running through Crossing/traversing A river running through the town center remained.
at the intersection of Where roads meet A roundabout was added at the intersection of Main St and Park Rd.

2.2 Transformation Vocabulary (Your Secret Weapon!)

These phrases show CHANGE – the heart of map descriptions. Memorize them!

Power Phrase Type of Change Band 7+ Example
was replaced by / was demolished and replaced by Complete substitution The farmland was replaced by a residential estate.
was converted into / was transformed into Changed purpose The old warehouse was converted into a museum.
was constructed / was built / was developed New addition A shopping center was constructed in the town center.
was extended / was expanded Made larger The parking lot was extended to accommodate more vehicles.
was removed / was demolished / disappeared Elimination The sports ground was removed to make way for housing.
remained unchanged / stayed the same No change The church in the center remained unchanged.
underwent significant development Major changes The eastern district underwent significant development.
was added / appeared / emerged New feature A bridge was added across the river.
CRITICAL MISTAKES TO AVOID
❌ Don't say: "They built a school" (Who is 'they'? Too casual!)
✅ Say: "A school was built" (Passive voice for maps!)
❌ Don't say: "The factory became a park"
✅ Say: "The factory was converted into a park"
❌ Don't say: "There were shops added"
✅ Say: "Shops were added" or "A row of shops was constructed"

3 PART 3: COMPARISON AND GROUPING STRUCTURES

Don't just list changes randomly! Group similar changes together and compare features. This shows analytical thinking – exactly what examiners want!

3.1 Grouping Similar Changes

TEMPLATE 1: Multiple similar changes

Several [types of building/facility] were [verb], including [example 1], [example 2], and [example 3].
EXAMPLE 5:
Several commercial facilities were constructed, including a shopping mall, a cinema complex, and a supermarket.

TEMPLATE 2: Emphasizing quantity

A significant number of / Numerous [buildings/facilities] were [verb] in the [location], making it more [characteristic].
EXAMPLE 6:
Numerous residential buildings were constructed in the western area, making it more densely populated.

3.2 Showing Contrast (Band 8+ Gold!)

These phrases show you're analyzing, not just describing. Use them to compare different parts of the map.

TEMPLATE 1: While + past simple (contrast)

While [change 1] in [location 1], [change 2] in [location 2].
EXAMPLE 7:
While the eastern side underwent extensive residential development, the western area remained largely agricultural.
GRAMMAR BREAKDOWN
While
Contrast marker → introduces opposite change
the eastern side
First location being compared
underwent
Past simple → describing completed changes
the western area
Second location → creates the contrast
remained
Shows lack of change → powerful contrast!

MORE CONTRAST STRUCTURES:

  • In contrast, the northern district saw minimal development.
  • Conversely, the southern area was transformed completely.
  • Whereas farmland dominated in 1990, residential zones had become the primary feature by 2020.
  • Unlike the western area which remained green, the east became heavily urbanized.

4 PART 4: TIME & SEQUENCE EXPRESSIONS

Maps show changes OVER TIME, so you need precise time expressions. Get these right for guaranteed marks!

4.1 Describing Time Periods

Time Phrase Example in Context
over the period / over the 30-year period The village expanded significantly over the 30-year period.
between [year] and [year] Between 1990 and 2020, the population doubled.
by [year] / by the end of [year] By 2020, three schools had been constructed.
during this time / during this period During this period, infrastructure was significantly improved.
throughout the timeframe Green spaces decreased throughout the timeframe.

4.2 Showing Sequence of Changes

When describing multiple changes in order, use these connectors:

  • Initially / At the beginning of the period, the area was predominantly rural.
  • Subsequently / Later, residential housing was constructed.
  • Towards the end of the period, commercial facilities were added.
  • By the final year, the transformation was complete.
TENSE MISTAKES TO AVOID
❌ Don't mix tenses: "The school was built and is used by students."
✅ Stay consistent: "The school was built in 2005." (All past simple for past changes)
❌ Wrong preposition: "In 1990 and 2020" (this means 2 separate points!)
✅ Correct: "Between 1990 and 2020" (showing a period)

5 PART 5: DATA & DETAIL INTEGRATION TECHNIQUES

Don't just name features – add DETAILS! This is what separates Band 6 from Band 8+.

5.1 Adding Specific Details

TEMPLATE: Feature + location + additional detail

[Feature] was [verb] in [specific location], [additional detail about size/purpose/connection].
EXAMPLE 8 (Band 6 → Band 8+):

Band 6 (basic): A bridge was built.

Band 7 (better): A bridge was constructed across the river.

Band 8+ (excellent): A bridge was constructed across the river in the northern section, connecting the residential area to the newly developed commercial district.

See the difference?
Band 8+ answers show HOW features relate to each other and their PURPOSE!

5.2 Combining Multiple Changes (Advanced!)

TEMPLATE: Complex sentence with multiple clauses

[Change 1], which [additional info], while [change 2] to [purpose/result].
EXAMPLE 9:
The farmland was replaced by a housing estate, which occupied approximately half of the available land, while the remainder was converted into a public park to provide recreational facilities for the new residents.

6 PUTTING IT ALL TOGETHER: MODEL ANSWER

Here's a complete Band 8+ response showing ALL the techniques in action. Study how the structures flow naturally!

Introduction:
The diagrams illustrate how Brandfield village has transformed over a 20-year period from 2000 to 2020. Overall, the area underwent substantial urbanization, with numerous residential buildings and facilities constructed, while the natural features such as the river and woodlands remained largely unchanged.
Body Paragraph 1:
In 2000, Brandfield was a predominantly rural village located adjacent to a river running through the center. The western side featured extensive farmland, while a small cluster of houses was situated in the northeastern corner. A single road connected the residential area to the main highway to the south.
Body Paragraph 2:
By 2020, significant development had occurred. The farmland to the west was replaced by a substantial residential estate, which now occupied approximately two-thirds of the available land. Additionally, several commercial facilities were constructed in the southern area, including a shopping complex and a leisure center. A new bridge was built across the river, connecting the residential zone to the newly developed commercial district, thereby improving accessibility. Conversely, the wooded area in the eastern section remained unchanged, preserving some of the village's original character.
💡 NOTICE HOW THIS ANSWER:
  • Uses passive voice consistently ("was replaced," "were constructed")
  • Groups similar changes together (commercial facilities)
  • Shows contrast ("Conversely")
  • Adds specific locations ("northeastern corner," "southern area")
  • Includes purpose/result ("thereby improving accessibility")
  • Maintains consistent past tense for historical changes
