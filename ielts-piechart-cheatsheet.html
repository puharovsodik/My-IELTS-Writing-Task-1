Top 10 Must-Use Structures
1
Introduction Paraphrase Band 6
The pie chart illustrates the breakdown of [topic] in [context].
2
Overview Statement Band 7
Overall, it is clear that [X] accounted for the largest proportion, while [Y] constituted the smallest share.
3
Dominant Segment Band 7
[X] dominated the chart, accounting for well over a third at 38%.
4
Grouping Two Segments Band 7
[X] and [Y] together accounted for nearly two-thirds of the total at 65%.
5
Ranked List (Smooth Flow) Band 7
[X] ranked highest at 38%, followed by [Y] (27%), [Z] (20%), and [W] (15%) in descending order.
6
Strong Contrast Band 8
Whereas [X] comprised approximately two-fifths, [Y] constituted a mere 15%.
7
Mathematical Comparison Band 8
[X]'s share was more than double that of [Y] (38% vs 15%), a stark contrast.
8
Passive Data Embed Band 8
65% of the total was directed towards [motorised transport], with the remaining 35% being divided between [X] and [Y].
9
Similar Categories Band 8
[X] and [Y] showed comparable proportions at 20% and 15% respectively.
10
Two-Chart Temporal Change Band 8
Between [Year A] and [Year B], [X]'s share expanded from 8% to 18% — more than double its original proportion.
⚡ Tense Rules — Never Get This Wrong
No DateUse present — "Cars account for 38%"
Past DateUse simple past — "Cars accounted for 38%"
2 ChartsPast both + change verbs between — "rose / expanded / contracted"
🚫 3 Instant Band-Killers
Cars increased to 38% → Cars accounted for 38% (single chart = no increase!)
38%, 27%, 20%, 15%... → Group + analyse, don't just list numbers.
The pie chart is about... → Paraphrase properly — never copy the task wording.

✅ Last-Minute Paragraph Structure
P1 Intro: Paraphrase topic + context. No stats yet.
P2 Overview: Biggest + smallest + one key grouping. No stats.
P3 Body 1: Top 2 segments with data + comparison.
P4 Body 2: Remaining segments + contrast + combined total.
