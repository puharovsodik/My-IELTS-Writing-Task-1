TOP 10 MUST-USE STRUCTURES 01–10
01
The bar chart [illustrates/compares/depicts] [what] in/across [scope].
→ The bar chart compares annual spending on education across seven nations.
02
Overall, [X] recorded the highest figure, while [Y] showed the lowest.
→ Overview sentence. No numbers. Highest + lowest only.
03
[Subject] stood at approximately [figure], which was [adj.] higher/lower than [Y].
→ Japan stood at ~85%, which was considerably higher than China.
04
[X] was approximately [ratio] times that of [Y].
→ US spending was roughly three times that of the UK.
05
While [X] [verb], [Y] [contrasting verb].
→ While men preferred outdoor sports, women favoured indoor activities.
06
[Group] — [A, B, C] — all exceeded/fell below [threshold].
→ The top three — Norway, Sweden, and Denmark — all exceeded 70%.
07
[X] and [Y] showed a broadly similar pattern, with both recording [range].
→ France and Germany showed a broadly similar pattern, both recording ~60%.
08
All categories [verb], with the notable exception of [X], which [contrasting verb].
→ All sectors grew, with the notable exception of agriculture, which contracted.
09
[X] [verb] from [value] to [value] between [year] and [year], [representing/marking] a [adj.] rise.
→ Imports rose from 20% to 45% between 2000 and 2020, marking a dramatic increase.
10
The disparity between the highest and lowest figures amounted to [X] [pp/units].
→ The disparity amounted to ~55 percentage points, underscoring a marked inequality.

🚫 DANGER ZONES
  • "The bar went up" → "recorded a higher figure"
  • "France is the highest" → "France recorded the highest figure"
  • "increased with 15%" → "increased by 15%"
  • "in between 2000 to 2020" → "between 2000 and 2020"
  • "very big/small" → "substantial / marginal"
  • Numbers in overview → Save data for body paragraphs
  • Copy question wording → Always paraphrase
✅ QUICK WINS
  • Group bars (top/mid/low), don't list every bar
  • Overview = no numbers. Pattern only.
  • Use "a mere X" to emphasise a low value
  • Use "% points" (not %) for the gap between two %s
  • Add "roughly/approximately" for estimated values
  • Rotate synonyms: rose → increased → climbed → surged
  • Target 165–185 words for Task 1
