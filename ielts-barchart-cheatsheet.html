Critical Mistakes Guide
TOP 10 MUST-USE STRUCTURES 01–10
01
The bar chart [illustrates/compares/depicts] [what] in/across [scope].
→ The bar chart compares annual spending on education across seven nations.
02
Overall, [X] recorded the highest figure, while [Y] showed the lowest.
→ Overview sentence. No numbers. Highest + lowest only.
03
[Subject] stood at approximately [figure], which was [adj.] higher/lower than [Y].
→ Japan stood at ~85%, which was considerably higher than China. [STATIC charts]
04
[X] was approximately [ratio] times that of [Y].
→ US spending was roughly three times that of the UK.
05
While [X] [verb], [Y] [contrasting verb].
→ While men preferred outdoor sports, women favoured indoor activities.
06
[Group] — [A, B, C] — all exceeded/fell below [threshold].
→ The top three — Norway, Sweden, and Denmark — all exceeded 70%.
07
[X] and [Y] showed a broadly similar pattern, with both recording [range].
→ France and Germany showed a broadly similar pattern, both recording ~60%.
08
All categories [verb], with the notable exception of [X], which [contrasting verb].
→ All sectors grew, with the notable exception of agriculture, which contracted.
09
[X] [verb] from [value] to [value] between [year] and [year], [representing/marking] a [adj.] rise.
→ Imports rose from 20% to 45% between 2000 and 2020, marking a dramatic increase. [DYNAMIC only]
10
The disparity between the highest and lowest figures amounted to [X] [pp/units].
→ The disparity amounted to ~55 percentage points, underscoring a marked inequality.

⚡ STATIC vs DYNAMIC — Know the Difference!
📊 STATIC (1 time point)
"Internet usage in 5 countries in 2020"
✓ USE:
  • recorded, stood at, showed, had
  • ranked highest/lowest
  • was approximately X times that of Y
  • fell within the X–Y range
  • in 2020, as of 2019
✗ NEVER:
  • increased, rose, grew, climbed
  • declined, fell, dropped
  • experienced, witnessed, underwent
  • from 2020, between X and Y
📈 DYNAMIC (multiple years)
"Spending from 2000 to 2020"
✓ USE:
  • rose, increased, grew, climbed
  • declined, fell, dropped
  • from X to Y (values & years)
  • between 2000 and 2020
  • over the period, during this span
⚠ KEY:
  • Describe CHANGE over time
  • Compare THEN vs NOW
  • Use temporal transitions
🚨 MOST COMMON MISTAKE:
Static chart shows "France: 60%" → DON'T say "increased to 60%" → SAY "stood at 60%" or "was 60%"
🚫 DANGER ZONES
  • "The bar went up" → "recorded a higher figure"
  • "France is higher" → "France recorded a higher figure"
  • "increased with 15%" → "increased by 15%"
  • "very big/small" → "substantial / negligible"
  • Copy question wording → Always paraphrase verbs
  • List every bar separately → Group them (high/mid/low)
✅ QUICK WINS
  • Check: Static or Dynamic? Use correct vocab!
  • Group bars (top/mid/low), don't list every bar
  • Overview = no numbers. Pattern only.
  • Use "a mere X" to emphasise low values
  • Use "% points" for gaps between percentages
  • Rotate synonyms: rose → increased → climbed
  • Target 165–185 words for Task 1
📌 PREPOSITIONS
inyear/decade — "in 2010", "in the 1990s"
between…andtwo points — "between 2000 and 2020"
from…tochange — "from 20% to 45%"
byamount of change — "increased by 15pp"
atspecific value — "stood at ~60%"
overwhole span — "over the entire period"
acrosscategories — "across all five nations"
